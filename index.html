Our Story

A small cafe with a big heart

Vikas Cafe began with one simple idea — Prathvipur Dhai deserved a corner where friends could talk for hours, families could laugh over hot snacks, and every cup felt like it was made just for you.

Today, from morning kulhad chai to late-night shakes, everything on our menu is made fresh, served warm, and priced honestly. This is your corner as much as ours.
