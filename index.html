Our Story

One kettle, one dream, and a corner of Prathvipur Dhai that refused to stay quiet.

☕

Vikas started with a secondhand kettle, six plastic chairs, and a stubborn belief that this town deserved a corner where nobody rushes you. The first month, he sold eleven cups of chai. He still remembers all eleven names.

Word travelled the way good chai does — slowly, then all at once. Students claimed the window counter, grandfathers claimed the morning sun, and someone's sister ordered our first birthday cake in month three. We've grown the menu, the chairs, and the speaker — never the hurry.

Today the kitchen turns out pizzas, momos and brownies alongside the pour-overs, but the rule from day one still hangs behind the counter: make it like you'd serve it to family, or don't serve it.

Meet Vikas

Eight years of pulling shots in other people's cafes taught Vikas one thing: a cafe is not a business with tables, it's a living room with a till. He brought that idea home to Prathvipur Dhai in 2021.

He still tastes every new syrup before it reaches your cup, still remembers the eleven names, and still closes most nights with one experimental dessert "for the regulars".

"A good cafe doesn't sell coffee. It sells an hour that belonged to you."
— Vikas
